# AutoBash 7.5 — easier to learn
Install over your existing version; do not uninstall, so presets remain.
Home now begins with three plain-language tasks and an Android access/setup card. Once access is available, that card opens a practice screen.
Every main tab has “How to use this · ?”. Guides show one step at a time, with Next, Back, Close and a topic index. Help never starts automation or enables Allow actions.
Settings groups have contextual guides; Auto Tap dialogs and the crop editor have help too. Preset sharing, matching, regions, speed and troubleshooting are covered in the guide index.
Advanced home/session details are folded to keep the main choices clear.
See USABILITY_7.5.md for iterative review, the 9.1/10 heuristic score and its limitations. No real-child or physical-device usability study was performed.
